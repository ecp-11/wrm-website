# Whispering River Media — Brand Guidelines

*Version 1.0 · May 2026*

---

## Brand Essence

**Mission:** Documentary storytelling at the intersection of earth science and human experience.

**Brand character:** Quiet authority, natural precision, earned trust.

**Visual tone:** Understated, matte, handcrafted-but-rigorous.

---

## Logo

**Primary asset:** `Simple_trsnprnt_Square.png` (4096×4096, transparent background)

### Clear space
Maintain minimum margin equal to the cap-height of the "W" on all four sides of the logo.

### Minimum sizes
- Digital: 200px wide
- Print: 1.5 inches wide

### Approved color versions

| Version | Use when |
|---------|----------|
| Black logo | On Parchment, Linen, or light backgrounds |
| White-reversed | On Charcoal or dark backgrounds |
| Stone (mid-grey) | On neutral or textured mid-tone backgrounds |

### Don'ts
- Do not stretch or distort
- Do not add drop shadows or glow effects
- Do not fill the illustration or letterforms with color
- Do not place in a background box or container shape
- Do not add outlines to the letterforms
- Do not use on backgrounds that reduce legibility

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|------|------|-----|
| Background | Parchment | `#F2EDE6` |
| White | Linen | `#FAF8F5` |
| Light grey | Ash | `#C4BFB8` |
| Mid grey | Stone | `#857F78` |
| Dark grey | Slate | `#44403B` |
| Near-black | Charcoal | `#1C1A17` |
| Accent | Sage | `#7D8C7A` |

### Rules
- Never use pure black (`#000000`) or pure white (`#FFFFFF`) — always use Charcoal or Linen
- Sage is an accent color — use sparingly, one use per layout maximum
- Primary text: Charcoal on Parchment or Linen
- Dark mode / film: Parchment text on Charcoal backgrounds

## Typography

The WRM wordmark uses **Josefin Sans** — a thin, geometric sans-serif with even stroke weight and clean proportions. **EB Garamond** provides warm serif contrast for body text. Both are available via Google Fonts.

| Role | Font | Weight |
|------|------|--------|
| Display / Headings | Josefin Sans | Thin (100) · Light (300) |
| Body | EB Garamond | Regular (400) · Medium (500) |
| Captions / Labels | Josefin Sans | Regular (400) |

### Type scale

| Level | Font | Size |
|-------|------|------|
| Display | Josefin Sans Thin | 48–72px |
| H1 | Josefin Sans Light | 32px |
| H2 | Josefin Sans Light | 24px |
| Body | EB Garamond Regular | 16–17px |
| Caption | Josefin Sans Regular | 11–12px, uppercase, +200 tracking |

### Rules
- Headlines: Josefin Sans Thin or Light, sentence case
- Use size hierarchy, not weight, to establish visual hierarchy
- Body: EB Garamond Regular (warm serif contrasts intentionally with the geometric sans)
- Captions and labels: Josefin Sans Regular, uppercase, wide letter-spacing
- Film title cards: Josefin Sans Thin on Charcoal or Parchment — fade-in only (no slide or zoom)
- Never bold or italicize headlines
