"The palette is dug from the earth rather than chosen. These tones are matte, light-absorbing, the opposite of spectacle. Color speaks through restraint: the eye rests, then notices."

Headlines: Josefin Sans Thin or Light, sentence case — never bold, never italic.
Use size hierarchy, not weight, to establish visual priority.
Body: EB Garamond Regular — warm serif contrasts intentionally with the geometric sans.
Captions and labels: Josefin Sans Regular, uppercase, wide letter-spacing (+200).
Film title cards: Josefin Sans Thin — fade-in only, no slide or zoom.
04 — Logo

Logo

The WRM mark used on light, dark, and neutral surfaces. Maintain clear space equal to the cap-height of the "W" on all four sides. Minimum size: 200px wide for digital, 1.5 inches for print.

05 — Brand Poster

Brand Poster

A museum-quality brand identity sheet — mountain mark, full color palette, typography note, and WRM monogram on a charcoal field. Use as a pitch deck cover, festival submission cover page, or internal reference.

06 — Icon Mark

Icon Mark

A compact mark for favicons, social profiles, and app icons where the full logo would be illegible. Use the Mountain Mark as the default; the Monogram is an alternative for contexts where the illustration reads as too complex.

Mountain Mark

Simplified mountain range with river lines and a tight WRM monogram. Recommended for most icon contexts.
