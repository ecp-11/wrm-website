III. — Practice

A studio
that listens
first.

Whispering River Media was founded by Ethan Parrish in 2019, after a decade spent moving between fieldwork and the editing room. The work attempts to bridge two disciplines that rarely speak the same language: the precision of earth science and the patience of documentary observation.

Every project begins with a question rooted in a specific place. The camera follows. Editing is a slow accumulation. Nothing is forced into resolution it does not have.
