What Whispering River Media does

Your research is important, that's why you do it.
I want to help you share it.

01 The Gap

One of the many things my background in academia taught me is that much of the incredible research that institutions around the world pursue never sees the light of day in the public's eye. More often than not, this is a function of the academic workload and the unfortunate fact that "public outreach", however important, is understandably not a priority relative to the actual research. That's where I come in. I'll work closely with you and your team to understand your work, why and how you do it, and its impact so that I can tell honest and authentic stories about you and your work.

02 How I Work

Given the nature of the myriad sources of funding, project scales, outreach objectives, etc., I'll work with you to define and execute projects in a way that makes sense for you, your research, and your vision.

03 Scale

From smaller web shorts to feature-length documentaries, I have the skill and/or collaborator connections to execute across multiple scales.
