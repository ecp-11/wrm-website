A Virtual Field Experience

Not mobile device compatible

Step into the (virtual) boots of a field geologist and explore one of the several sandstone outcrops of the Green River Formation in southwest Wyoming that informs my Ph.D. research.

Directions

Use the W A S D keys to move through the environment, and right-click on a mouse or trackpad to change direction (I recommend using a mouse). With “jetpack” enabled in the bottom left-hand corner, use the E and Q keys to go up and down.
